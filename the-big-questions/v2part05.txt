t freedom, responsibility and authenticity may all change. Philosophy therefore often revises networks of concepts, not isolated definitions.</p><p class="zh">信念不是一个个孤岛。假如你把“自我”从独立内核改成“由关系与社会共同构成”，你对自由、责任、真实自我的理解都会一起变化。所以哲学经常改的是整套概念网络。</p></article></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>02 · POSITIONS</small><h3>What the rival views actually claim <span>竞争立场到底在说什么</span></h3></div>
  <div class="position-grid"><div class="position"><b>Deduction</b><span>演绎</span><p class="en">If the premises are true and the form is valid, the conclusion must be true.</p><p class="zh">如果前提为真且推理有效，结论必然为真。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Induction</b><span>归纳</span><p class="en">Evidence raises or lowers probability but does not guarantee the conclusion.</p><p class="zh">证据提高或降低结论的概率，但不能保证结论。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Counterexample</b><span>反例</span><p class="en">A single well-designed case can expose a definition or universal claim as too broad or too narrow.</p><p class="zh">一个设计得好的反例，就可能证明某个定义或普遍命题太宽或太窄。</p></div></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>03 · ARGUMENT WORKSHOP</small><h3>Reconstruct the reasoning <span>把论证一步步还原</span></h3></div>
  <div class="argbox"><div class="argstep"><i>Step 1</i><div><p class="en">Write a belief in one sentence without using a vague virtue-word as a substitute for explanation.</p><p class="zh">不用“自由、真实、意义”这类大词糊弄过去，用一句话写出你真正相信什么。</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>Step 2</i><div><p class="en">List the assumptions the sentence depends on. Ask what would have to be true for your belief to work.</p><p class="zh">列出这个信念依赖的前提：要让它成立，世界必须是什么样？人必须是什么样？</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>Step 3</i><div><p class="en">Imagine the strongest reasonable objection, not the easiest opponent to defeat.</p><p class="zh">不要找最蠢的反方，要主动构造最强的反对意见。</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>Step 4</i><div><p class="en">Revise the claim so that it survives the objection without becoming empty.</p><p class="zh">修改主张，让它能承受反驳，同时又不能退化成“永远不会错”的空话。</p></div></div></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>04 · OBJECTIONS</small><h3>Where the view gets pressured <span>真正困难在哪里</span></h3></div>
  <div class="objections"><div class="objection"><b>“Philosophy is just opinion.”</b><p class="en">If every answer were merely personal preference, reasons and contradictions would not matter. Yet we routinely distinguish a coherent position from one that defeats itself.</p><p class="zh">“哲学只是个人意见。”——如果真是如此，理由和自相矛盾就不重要。但现实中我们显然会区分“有理由的立场”和“自己把自己推翻的立场”。</p></div><div class="objection"><b>“There is no final answer, so why bother?”</b><p class="en">A question can matter even without a final solution. Better distinctions change decisions, institutions and how we interpret experience.</p><p class="zh">“又没有终极答案，想它干嘛？”——没有终局不等于没有进步。更好的概念区分会改变决策、制度和我们理解经历的方式。</p></div></div>
