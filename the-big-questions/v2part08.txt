</article><article class="concept"><h4>Narrative identity / 叙事身份</h4><p class="en">To see life as a story is to organize events into a plot: beginnings, turning points, failures, development and possible endings. Identity becomes partly the story through which we interpret change.</p><p class="zh">把人生当故事，就是把事件组织成情节：开端、转折、失败、成长、结局。于是“我是谁”部分取决于我如何把变化串成一个故事。</p></article><article class="concept"><h4>Metaphor as norm / 隐喻即规范</h4><p class="en">If life is a game, winning matters; if a mission, fidelity to purpose matters; if art, creation matters; if relationship, connection matters. A metaphor does not merely describe life—it tells you what to praise and regret.</p><p class="zh">如果人生是游戏，胜负重要；如果是使命，忠于目标重要；如果是艺术，创造重要；如果是关系，连接重要。隐喻不只是描述人生，也在告诉你什么值得赞赏、什么算失败。</p></article></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>02 · POSITIONS</small><h3>What the rival views actually claim <span>竞争立场到底在说什么</span></h3></div>
  <div class="position-grid"><div class="position"><b>Given meaning</b><span>被给予的意义</span><p class="en">Meaning comes from God, destiny, family duty, tradition or a larger order.</p><p class="zh">意义来自上帝、命运、家庭义务、传统或更大的秩序。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Created meaning</b><span>被创造的意义</span><p class="en">Meaning is made through projects, commitments, relationships and interpretation.</p><p class="zh">意义通过项目、承诺、关系与解释被创造。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Nihilism</b><span>虚无主义</span><p class="en">There is no objective or ultimate meaning; stronger versions infer that nothing genuinely matters.</p><p class="zh">不存在客观终极意义；更强版本进一步认为因此什么都不真正重要。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Pluralism</b><span>多元主义</span><p class="en">Lives can be meaningful in several irreducible ways—love, creation, knowledge, service, beauty, struggle.</p><p class="zh">人生可以在多个不可互相化约的维度上有意义：爱、创造、知识、服务、美、抗争。</p></div></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>03 · ARGUMENT WORKSHOP</small><h3>Reconstruct the reasoning <span>把论证一步步还原</span></h3></div>
  <div class="argbox"><div class="argstep"><i>1</i><div><p class="en">The question &#x27;What is the meaning of life?&#x27; is too vague until &#x27;meaning&#x27; is disambiguated.</p><p class="zh">“生命的意义是什么”太含糊，必须先拆开 meaning。</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>2</i><div><p class="en">Local purposes—finish a degree, raise a child, build a career—do not automatically answer the whole-life question.</p><p class="zh">毕业、养孩子、做事业等局部目标，并不会自动回答整个人生为何值得。</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>3</i><div><p class="en">External sources can ground a life, but they invite regress: why is serving God, family or history itself meaningful?</p><p class="zh">外部来源能赋予结构，但会继续追问：为什么侍奉上帝、家庭或历史本身就有意义？</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>4</i><div><p class="en">The metaphor route avoids one single cosmic answer and asks instead which form of life one can endorse.</p><p class="zh">隐喻路径绕开单一宇宙答案，转而问：你愿意认可哪种人生形式？</p></div></div></div>
