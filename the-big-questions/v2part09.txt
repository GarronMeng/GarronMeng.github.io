  <div class="concept-grid"><article class="concept"><h4>Transcendence / 超越</h4><p class="en">God is fundamentally beyond the world and not reducible to nature.</p><p class="zh">上帝根本上超越世界，不能还原成自然本身。</p></article><article class="concept"><h4>Immanence / 内在</h4><p class="en">The divine is present in or through the world rather than wholly outside it.</p><p class="zh">神圣性存在于世界之中或贯穿世界，而非完全在世界之外。</p></article><article class="concept"><h4>Theodicy / 神义论</h4><p class="en">A theodicy tries to reconcile divine goodness and power with the existence of evil and suffering.</p><p class="zh">神义论试图调和“上帝全善全能”与“世界存在恶和苦难”。</p></article><article class="concept"><h4>Faith / 信仰</h4><p class="en">Faith can mean confidence, commitment or trust where demonstrative proof is unavailable; the philosophical issue is whether such commitment can be rational.</p><p class="zh">faith 可以指在无法演绎证明时的信任、承诺与确信；哲学问题是：这样的承诺是否仍可理性。</p></article></div>
