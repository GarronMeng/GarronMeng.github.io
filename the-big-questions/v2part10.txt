position-grid"><div class="position"><b>Classical theism</b><span>经典有神论</span><p class="en">God is omnipotent, omniscient, perfectly good and creator.</p><p class="zh">上帝全能、全知、全善并创造世界。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Deism</b><span>自然神论</span><p class="en">God creates or grounds the universe but need not intervene continuously.</p><p class="zh">上帝创造或奠定宇宙，却未必持续干预。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Pantheism</b><span>泛神论</span><p class="en">God and nature/world are in some sense identical.</p><p class="zh">上帝与自然/世界在某种意义上同一。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Agnosticism</b><span>不可知论</span><p class="en">The available grounds do not justify either confident belief or confident denial.</p><p class="zh">现有根据不足以支持坚定相信，也不足以支持坚定否认。</p></div></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>03 · ARGUMENT WORKSHOP</small><h3>Reconstruct the reasoning <span>把论证一步步还原</span></h3></div>
  <div class="argbox"><div class="argstep"><i>Problem of evil</i><div><p class="en">If God is able to prevent pointless suffering, knows it occurs, and is perfectly good, why is there apparently pointless suffering?</p><p class="zh">如果上帝有能力阻止无意义的苦难、知道苦难发生、而且全善，为什么仍有看似无意义的苦难？</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>Free-will defense</i><div><p class="en">Significant freedom may require the real possibility of wrongdoing; preventing every misuse would undercut the gift.</p><p class="zh">自由意志辩护：有分量的自由可能必须包含真正做恶的可能；若上帝阻止每次滥用，自由本身会被掏空。</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>Cosmological route</i><div><p class="en">Contingent things appear to require explanation; the argument seeks a necessary or first ground rather than an endless regress.</p><p class="zh">宇宙论路径：偶然存在者似乎需要解释，于是论证寻找一个必要存在或第一根据，避免无限回溯。</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>Design route</i><div><p class="en">Order, complexity or apparent purposiveness is taken as evidence of intelligence, though evolutionary explanation changes the force of some traditional forms.</p><p class="zh">设计论路径：秩序、复杂性与目的感被视为智能的证据；进化论会削弱某些传统版本，但不会自动解决所有设计型问题。</p></div></div></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>04 · OBJECTIONS</small><h3>Where the view gets pressured <span>真正困难在哪里</span></h3></div>
  <div class="objections"><div class="objection"><b>Natural evil</b><p class="en">Free will can explain murder more easily than earthquakes, congenital disease or suffering unrelated to human choice.</p><p class="zh">自然恶：自由意志更容易解释谋杀，却难解释地震、先天疾病等与人的选择无关的苦难。</p></div><div class="objection"><b>Who explains the explainer?</b><p class="en">If everything needs a cause, why does God not? The cosmological argument therefore must distinguish contingent things from a purported necessary ground.</p><p class="zh">“那谁解释上帝？”——如果一切都要有原因，上帝为何例外？宇宙论必须区分偶然存在与所谓必要根据，而不能只说“凡事都有原因”。</p></div></div>
  <div class="thought"><div class="layer-label">THOUGHT EXPERIMENT · 思想实验</div><p class="en">Suppose a world contains no suffering because every harmful choice is automatically blocked. Are the inhabitants morally free? Now suppose suffering exists but serves no discernible good. Which world is more compatible with classical theism?</p><p class="zh">想象一个没有苦难的世界：任何伤害他人的选择都会自动被系统拦截。这里的人还道德自由吗？再想象存在无法看出任何善果的苦难。哪种世界更容易与经典有神论相容？</p></div>
