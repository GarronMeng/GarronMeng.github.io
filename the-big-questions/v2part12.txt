>心灵、经验或观念对“可知世界”甚至现实本身具有构成作用。</p></div></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>03 · ARGUMENT WORKSHOP</small><h3>Reconstruct the reasoning <span>把论证一步步还原</span></h3></div>
  <div class="argbox"><div class="argstep"><i>From change to permanence</i><div><p class="en">If everything we encounter changes, how can knowledge have stable objects? One move is to posit a more stable level of reality.</p><p class="zh">从变化到稳定：如果所有可见事物都变化，知识如何拥有稳定对象？一种做法是提出更稳定的现实层次。</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>Against a separate realm</i><div><p class="en">Aristotle&#x27;s answer is that form must explain the concrete thing without being exiled to another world.</p><p class="zh">反对独立理念界：亚里士多德要让“形式”解释具体事物，却不把它放逐到另一个世界。</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>Mind as problem</i><div><p class="en">Once knowledge is mediated by consciousness, philosophers ask whether matter alone can explain experience or whether mind has irreducible status.</p><p class="zh">心灵问题：既然所有知识都经由意识，哲学家会问物质是否足以解释体验，还是心灵具有不可还原地位。</p></div></div></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>04 · OBJECTIONS</small><h3>Where the view gets pressured <span>真正困难在哪里</span></h3></div>
  <div class="objections"><div class="objection"><b>Against reduction</b><p class="en">Reducing love to hormones, law to physical marks, or money to paper may miss the level at which these things actually function and matter.</p><p class="zh">反对过度还原：把爱还原成激素、法律还原成纸上墨迹、货币还原成纸张，可能错过它们真正发挥作用的层次。</p></div><div class="objection"><b>Against multiplying entities</b><p class="en">Positing invisible Forms or metaphysical substances can explain by adding entities whose own status then needs explanation.</p><p class="zh">反对实体膨胀：为了说明世界而增加不可见理念或形而上实体，可能只是把解释责任推给新的东西。</p></div></div>
  <div class="thought"><div class="layer-label">THOUGHT EXPERIMENT · 思想实验</div><p class="en">Is a corporation real? It has no single body, yet it owns property, signs contracts and can outlive every employee. Compare its reality with a chair, a number and a friendship.</p><p class="zh">“公司”真实吗？它没有一个单一身体，却能拥有财产、签合同、比所有员工活得更久。把它与椅子、数字、友谊的“真实方式”比较。</p></div>
  <div class="next"><b>Why the next chapter follows · 为什么下一章会接在这里</b><p class="en">Once you decide what reality is like, Chapter 5 asks the unavoidable next question: how could we know it?</p><p class="zh">一旦你开始回答“现实是什么”，第 5 章立刻追问：我们怎么知道？</p></div>
