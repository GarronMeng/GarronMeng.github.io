small>01 · CONCEPTS</small><h3>Conceptual deep dive <span>概念深挖</span></h3></div>
  <div class="concept-grid"><article class="concept"><h4>Truth / 真理</h4><p class="en">Truth concerns how things are; justification concerns why a person is entitled to believe a claim. A belief can be justified yet false, or true by luck without being knowledge.</p><p class="zh">真理关乎事情本身如何；正当化关乎一个人凭什么相信。一个信念可能“有充分理由但实际为假”，也可能“碰巧为真却不算知识”。</p></article><article class="concept"><h4>Empirical vs. necessary / 经验与必然</h4><p class="en">Empirical claims depend on how the world happens to be; necessary truths are presented as not capable of being otherwise within the relevant framework.</p><p class="zh">经验命题取决于世界事实上怎样；必然真理则被理解为在相关框架中“不可能不是这样”。</p></article><article class="concept"><h4>Skepticism / 怀疑论</h4><p class="en">Skepticism asks whether our evidence is strong enough for the confidence we claim. Radical skepticism questions whether perception, memory or induction can justify much at all.</p><p class="zh">怀疑论追问：证据是否真的配得上我们的确信。激进怀疑甚至会怀疑感知、记忆与归纳究竟能证明多少。</p></article><article class="concept"><h4>Rationality / 理性</h4><p class="en">The chapter emphasizes good reasons, relevance, evidence and coherence. Rationality is a norm for belief even when truth is not guaranteed.</p><p class="zh">本章强调好理由、相关性、证据与融贯。理性是一种信念规范，即使它不能保证真理。</p></article></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>02 · POSITIONS</small><h3>What the rival views actually claim <span>竞争立场到底在说什么</span></h3></div>
  <div class="position-grid"><div class="position"><b>Rationalism</b><span>理性主义</span><p class="en">Reason and a priori structure are indispensable sources of knowledge.</p><p class="zh">理性与先验结构是知识不可缺的来源。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Empiricism</b><span>经验主义</span><p class="en">Experience is the primary source and test of substantive knowledge.</p><p class="zh">经验是实质知识的主要来源与检验。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Coherence theory</b><span>融贯论</span><p class="en">A belief&#x27;s credibility depends partly on how well it fits a wider web of beliefs.</p><p class="zh">信念的可信度部分取决于它与更大信念网络是否一致互撑。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Pragmatism</b><span>实用主义</span><p class="en">The practical fruitfulness of a belief or hypothesis matters to how we assess it.</p><p class="zh">一个信念或假设是否能推动实践、检验与探究，也是评价因素。</p></div></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>03 · ARGUMENT WORKSHOP</small><h3>Reconstruct the reasoning <span>把论证一步步还原</span></h3></div>
  <div class="argbox"><div class="argstep"><i>Descartes</i><div><p class="en">Doubt everything that can be doubted, then see whether anything survives as a secure starting point.</p><p class="zh">笛卡尔：把所有可怀疑的东西都怀疑一遍，看是否仍有无法动摇的起点。</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>Hume</i><div><p class="en">Experience gives repeated conjunction, not logical necessity; induction assumes future regularity that experience itself cannot non-circularly prove.</p><p class="zh">休谟：经验只给出反复相随，却不给逻辑必然；归纳假设未来继续像过去，而经验无法不循环地证明这个假设。</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>Kant</i><div><p class="en">Instead of asking only what we receive from the world, ask what structures the mind must contribute for coherent experience to be possible.</p><p class="zh">康德：不只问世界给了我们什么，还问心灵必须提供什么结构，经验才可
