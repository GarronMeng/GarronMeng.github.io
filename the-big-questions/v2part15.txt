  <div class="section-title"><small>02 · POSITIONS</small><h3>What the rival views actually claim <span>竞争立场到底在说什么</span></h3></div>
  <div class="position-grid"><div class="position"><b>Substance self</b><span>实体自我</span><p class="en">A persistent inner subject or soul grounds identity.</p><p class="zh">持续存在的内在主体/灵魂奠定同一性。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Psychological continuity</b><span>心理连续论</span><p class="en">Memory, intentions, character and connected mental life ground identity.</p><p class="zh">记忆、意图、性格及心理连接构成同一性。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Functionalism</b><span>功能主义</span><p class="en">Mental states are understood by the roles they play in a system rather than by a special inner substance.</p><p class="zh">心灵状态通过其在系统中的功能角色理解，而非某种特殊内在实体。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Relational / process self</b><span>关系/过程自我</span><p class="en">Selfhood is enacted through changing processes, choices and relationships rather than discovered as a fixed core.</p><p class="zh">自我通过过程、选择与关系被实践，而不是发现一个固定核心。</p></div></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>03 · ARGUMENT WORKSHOP</small><h3>Reconstruct the reasoning <span>把论证一步步还原</span></h3></div>
  <div class="argbox"><div class="argstep"><i>Memory test</i><div><p class="en">If memory alone makes identity, would a perfect copy with all your memories be you? If two copies exist, can both be numerically identical to one original?</p><p class="zh">记忆测试：如果记忆就是同一性，那么拥有你全部记忆的完美复制体是你吗？若同时有两个复制体，它们都能与原来的你“数值上同一”吗？</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>Body test</i><div><p class="en">If your psychology were transferred to another body, which criterion would you trust—body or mind?</p><p class="zh">身体测试：如果你的心理被转移到另一个身体，你更相信身体标准还是心理标准？</p></div></div><div class="argstep"><i>Social turn</i><div><p class="en">Many self-descriptions have meaning only against shared social norms; therefore an entirely private self may be philosophically thinner than it first appears.</p><p class="zh">社会转向：很多自我描述只有放进共享规范中才有意义，因此“完全私人化的自我”可能比直觉中更空。</p></div></div></div>
