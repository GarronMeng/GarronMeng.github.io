    <div><div class="pages">pp. 220–245</div><h2>Freedom<span>自由</span></h2>
    <div class="question"><p class="en">What kind of freedom is required for agency and responsibility?</p><p class="zh">要让“能动性与责任”成立，我们究竟需要哪种自由？</p></div></div>
  </header>
  <div class="book-layer"><div class="layer-label">BOOK-ANCHORED · 原著基底</div><p class="en">The chapter distinguishes political freedom from metaphysical free will, links freedom to concepts of self, stages determinism against indeterminism, then introduces compatibilism as a redefinition of what freedom may require.</p><p class="zh">本章先区分政治自由与形而上自由意志，再把自由与自我概念连接，展开决定论/非决定论争论，最后用相容论重新定义“自由究竟需要什么”。</p></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>01 · CONCEPTS</small><h3>Conceptual deep dive <span>概念深挖</span></h3></div>
  <div class="concept-grid"><article class="concept"><h4>Political liberty / 政治自由</h4><p class="en">Freedom from legal or social interference: speech, movement, association, bodily autonomy and political participation.</p><p class="zh">免于法律或社会干预的自由，包括言论、迁徙、结社、身体自主与政治参与。</p></article><article class="concept"><h4>Determinism / 决定论</h4><p class="en">Every event, including human action, is fully determined by prior conditions and laws.</p><p class="zh">每个事件，包括人的行动，都由先前条件与规律充分决定。</p></article><article class="concept"><h4>Indeterminism / 非决定论</h4><p class="en">Not every event is determined by the past; however, mere randomness does not yet equal agency.</p><p class="zh">并非所有事件都由过去决定；但“随机”本身还不等于能动性。</p></article><article class="concept"><h4>Compatibilism / 相容论</h4><p class="en">Freedom can coexist with causal determination if freedom means acting from one&#x27;s own reasons and desires without coercion or certain forms of compulsion.</p><p class="zh">如果自由意味着依据自己的理由和欲望行动，并且没有受到强制或某些形式的控制，那么自由可以与因果决定共存。</p></article></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>02 · POSITIONS</small><h3>What the rival views actually claim <span>竞争立场到底在说什么</span></h3></div>
  <div class="position-grid"><div class="position"><b>Hard determinism</b><span>强决定论</span><p class="en">Determinism is true and robust free will is not.</p><p class="zh">决定论为真，强意义自由意志不存在。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Libertarianism</b><span>自由意志论</span><p class="en">At least some choices are genuinely open and not fully determined by prior causes.</p><p class="zh">至少部分选择真正开放，不被先前原因完全决定。</p></div><div class="position"><b>
