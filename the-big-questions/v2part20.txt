>Rights / 权利</h4><p class="en">Rights protect claims or liberties against other people and institutions; debates concern their basis, scope and conflicts.</p><p class="zh">权利保护个人对他人或制度的主张/自由；争议在于其根据、范围与冲突。</p></article><article class="concept"><h4>Structural injustice / 结构性不正义</h4><p class="en">Systematic disadvantage can arise from institutions, history and norms even without one malicious individual intentionally causing every harm.</p><p class="zh">系统性劣势可以来自制度、历史与规范，即使没有一个恶意个人故意造成所有伤害。</p></article></div>
  <div class="section-title"><small>02 · POSITIONS</small><h3>What the rival views actually claim <span>竞争立场到底在说什么</span></h3></div>
  <div class="position-grid"><div class="position"><b>Libertarianism</b><span>自由至上主义</span><p class="en">Strong property and individual liberty sharply restrict redistribution and state coercion.</p><p class="zh">强财产权与个人自由大幅限制再分配和国家强制。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Liberalism</b><span>自由主义</span><p class="en">Basic rights and liberties matter, but fair opportunity and institutional design may justify redistribution or regulation.</p><p class="zh">基本权利与自由重要，但公平机会和制度设计可以为再分配/监管提供正当性。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Communitarianism</b><span>共同体主义</span><p class="en">People are socially embedded; common goods, civic practices and membership shape justice.</p><p class="zh">人嵌入共同体，共同善、公民实践与成员身份共同塑造正义。</p></div><div class="position"><b>Anarchism</b><span>无政府主义</span><p class="en">Political authority itself requires justification; some versions deny that states possess a special moral right to command.</p><p class="zh">政治权威本身需要证明；某些无政府主义否认国家拥有特殊的道德命令权。</p></div></div>
